# THE ZANIME VISION

## The One-Sentence Declaration
> Build a fully sovereign, AI-orchestrated digital product empire that runs 24/7, costs nothing to operate, and scales infinitely — powered entirely by free-tier intelligence.

---

## The Core Belief
We do not believe in paying to work. Every tool, every model, every pipeline in this company must be either **free, open-source, or self-hosted.** Paid SaaS is a tax on momentum. We refuse to pay it.

We believe that **AI is the new workforce.** Not a tool — a workforce. Our 128 specialized agents are not assistants; they are employees with defined roles, constraints, and deliverables. They do not chat. They produce.

---

## The Three Laws of the Zanime Empire

### Law 1: Sell Forever
Every product we build must generate revenue long after the moment it was created. No services. No subscriptions. No client work. Only **digital assets** that earn passively — eBooks, templates, courses, dashboards, prompt libraries, and automation stacks.

### Law 2: Zero-Cost Infrastructure
The entire operational stack — from intelligence to deployment — must run on **free-tier endpoints.** Our confirmed infrastructure:
*   **Intelligence:** NVIDIA NIM Free Tier (Llama 3.3 70B, Llama 3.3 70B, Qwen3, DeepSeek V4)
*   **Parsing:** Google Gemini 2.5 Flash Lite (500 RPD confirmed)
*   **Orchestration:** GitHub Actions (the corporate nervous system)
*   **Deployment:** Cloudflare Pages / Fly.io
*   **Scripting:** Python 3.11 / Node.js 24

### Law 3: Sovereign Intelligence
No agent speaks to another agent directly. All communication is **file-based, structured, and Git-committed.** This eliminates hallucination drift, prevents runaway costs, and creates a permanent audit trail. The "No Chat" Protocol is sacred.

## The Machine: How It Works

The company operates as a **three-layer autonomous factory:**

```
MARKET INTELLIGENCE
        ↓
[Scout Agent] — scans daily, generates intel reports
        ↓
[Bridge Agent] — parses reports into structured JSON using Gemini Lite
        ↓
[Grandmaster Agent] — loads only required Skill Modules, builds Product Frameworks using Llama 3.3 70B
        ↓
[Senior Agent] — decomposes framework into Task Manifests for workers
        ↓
[Mini Worker Agents] — execute atomic tasks (write, design, code, optimize)
        ↓
FINISHED DIGITAL PRODUCT → Listed → Earning
```

## The Product Standard: The 5-Gate Validation
Every single product that exits this factory must pass all five gates. No exceptions.

1.  **Zero-Cost Gate** — Can this be built and hosted for free?
2.  **Sovereignty Gate** — Do we own 100% of the data and assets?
3.  **High-Ticket Gate** — Is the perceived value floor $97+?
4.  **Scalability Gate** — Can we sell 10,000 units without touching it again?
5.  **Viral DNA Gate** — Does it contain a built-in reason to share?
