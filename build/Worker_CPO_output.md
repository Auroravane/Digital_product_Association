**Prototype Demo: AI-Generated Legal Contract Templates**

Our AI-generated legal contract template tool, dubbed "ContractGenie," utilizes natural language processing (NLP) and machine learning algorithms to generate customized legal contracts based on user input. The tool aims to streamline the contract creation process, reducing the need for manual drafting and review by legal professionals.

**Prototype Features:**

1. **Contract Type Selection**: Users can choose from a variety of contract types, such as employment agreements, non-disclosure agreements, and lease agreements.
2. **Questionnaire-Based Input**: Users answer a series of questions related to the contract, such as party information, contract duration, and specific terms.
3. **AI-Generated Contract**: The tool generates a customized contract based on the user's input, using a combination of pre-built templates and machine learning algorithms.
4. **Review and Editing**: Users can review and edit the generated contract, with options to add or remove clauses, and modify language.
5. **Export and Sharing**: Users can export the final contract in various formats (e.g., PDF, Word) and share it with other parties.
